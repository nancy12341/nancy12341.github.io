It's like having a direct conversation with your machine! - **C99 and NASM**: These are programming languages used to build BooleOS. C99 is a modern version of the C language, known for its power and efficiency. NASM is an assembler, which translates low-level instructions that the CPU understands. - **Cathode Graphics API**: Think of it as BooleOS's own toolset for drawing and creating graphics. Currently a work in progress, it promises to make BooleOS visually exciting. --- ## 🛠️ System Requirements While BooleOS is lightweight, here are the minimum requirements to ensure smooth operation: - **CPU**: Intel or AMD x86 32-bit or 64-bit processor - **RAM**: At least 64 MB - **Storage**: 50 MB free disk space - **Display**: VGA compatible graphics card (for Cathode) - **Keyboard**: Standard USB or PS/2 keyboard These are conservative estimates—BooleOS is designed to run on modest hardware, so even older machines can handle it. --- ## 🤝 Join the Community BooleOS is more than just software; it's a collaborative effort fueled by passion.
